Where these figures come from

Stamp duty

  • IRAS — Buyer’s Stamp Duty (BSD)The 1–6% banded rates effective 20 Feb 2023, used to compute the duty.
  • IRAS — Stamp duty ratesCurrent rate tables, including ABSD (not modelled here).

Grants

  • HDB — CPF Housing Grants for resale flatsFamily Grant and EHG amounts and eligibility for first-timer families.
  • HDB — Proximity Housing GrantThe 4 km rule and the amounts for living with or near parents.

Loan limits

  • MAS — MSR and TDSR rulesMSR capped at 30% for HDB flats and new ECs; TDSR at 55%.
  • MAS — Loan tenure and LTV limitsThe 75%/55% LTV tiers and the tenure and age-65 tests.

CPF and lease

  • CPF Board — Home ownershipUsing OA savings for property, and the Valuation Limit.
  • CPF Board — Member servicesCheck your actual OA balance before relying on the figure entered here.

Map and distance

  • OneMapSingapore Land Authority basemap and address data.
  • OneMap API documentationThe search endpoint that turns a postal code into coordinates.
  • LeafletThe open-source library rendering the map.

Estimates only. BSD tiers are the rates effective 20 Feb 2023. MSR is capped at 30% and TDSR at 55% of gross monthly income, assessed at the higher of 4% p.a. or the prevailing rate. The age-95 lease pro-ration applies to CPF usage on any loan, and to LTV on an HDB concessionary loan only. Grant amounts, LTV limits and lease rules change — confirm with HDB, CPF Board, MAS and IRAS before committing. Distance is straight-line, the basis HDB uses for the 4 km proximity condition.

Not included: option fee and deposit, conveyancing and legal fees, HDB resale application fees, valuation, agent commission, resale levy and cash-over-valuation.
